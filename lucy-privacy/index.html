Lucy – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Lucy is a personal voice assistant built and used only by Luis Emanuel Daclag on his own smart mirror and computer. It is not a public service and has no other users.

What Lucy accesses

With the owner's permission, Lucy uses Google sign-in to read and add events in his Google Calendar and to read and update his Google Tasks lists. It uses this only to answer his own requests, such as "what's on my calendar today" or "add milk to my shopping list".

Where data goes

  • Sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's own device.
  • Calendar and task data is shown on his mirror and is not stored on any server, sold, or shared with anyone.
  • Google user data is not used for advertising or to train AI models.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from myaccount.google.com/permissions.
